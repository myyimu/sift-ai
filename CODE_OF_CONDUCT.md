# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Project participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and mindful
that privacy and security work can involve sensitive reports. Examples of
positive behavior include:

- giving actionable, evidence-based feedback;
- respecting differing experience, language, and background;
- accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes;
- protecting reporters and users from unnecessary disclosure;
- keeping discussions focused on the project and its documented boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private data or
credentials, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and
other spaces where someone represents the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior violates these standards. Enforcement will
consider context, impact, and the need to protect affected people.

For a private conduct or safety report, use the repository's private reporting
form and prefix the title with `[conduct]`:

https://github.com/myyimu/sift-ai/security/advisories/new

Do not include unrelated captured web content, credentials, or personal data.
